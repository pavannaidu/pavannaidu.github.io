The Royal Enfield Classic 350 (2012 model) is a motorcycle known for its classic styling and simple, reliable engine. It is a good choice for riders who are looking for a comfortable and stylish motorcycle for cruising around town or commuting.

This picture was taken near [Rameswaram, India](https://maps.app.goo.gl/CKa1R8gqgfNjqQRh9) in November 2016, during a south-indian-coastal roadtrip covering 2400Kms.
